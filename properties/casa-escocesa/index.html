Casa Escocesa

The gathering house of Tankah Bay

PriceUSD 2,195,000

A classic beach estate in the hacienda tradition, set on titled land at the quiet northern end of Tankah Bay with 20 metres of Caribbean frontage. Arches, terracotta roofs and a round glass tower room frame the sea, while about 4,000 sq ft of interiors flow from a gourmet kitchen and wet bar to the dining and principal living rooms. Four ensuite bedrooms, an upper media lounge and a dedicated workspace leave room for extended family and long stays.

Highlights

  • 20 m of Caribbean beachfront on titled land
  • Ocean-side pool of approx. 30 ft and beachfront palapa
  • Four ensuite bedrooms plus powder room
  • Approx. 1,000 sq ft of covered terraces and a rooftop terrace
  • Media lounge, workspace and individual air conditioning
