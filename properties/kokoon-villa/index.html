A fully furnished, detached three-level villa in Kokoon Pueblo, a boutique gated community of just eight villas and two apartments where around 65% of the original jungle has been preserved. Every bedroom has its own ensuite; the private rooftop adds a plunge pool, pergola and outdoor kitchen.
