Branded Residences · Brand Mythologies · No. 1

Five Rituals, One Coast

By Jorge Ángel Mejía · 2026-10-28

A boy left his stuffed giraffe at a Ritz-Carlton. Four days later it came home with a photo album: by the pool, at the spa, working a shift in Loss Prevention, staff badge included.

Nobody remembers the room rate. Everybody remembers the giraffe.

Five brand rituals

  • The Ritz-Carlton and the permission to care.
  • The St. Regis saber at dusk.
  • Mandarin Oriental's fans.
  • Mondrian and the building that changed its name.
  • Sofitel and culture as a service.

Why it matters to buyers

Every one of these brands is present or arriving in Quintana Roo. The story is part of what a branded residence sells, which is exactly why it deserves scrutiny before a client falls in love with a legend.

A brand's mythology is an asset only if the operator keeps delivering it after closing.
