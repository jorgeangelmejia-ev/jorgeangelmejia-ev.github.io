What Bosco Sodi Knows About Luxury

By Jorge Ángel Mejía · 2026-10-24

What does Bosco Sodi know about luxury that most real estate developers do not? That the most extraordinary things cannot always be manufactured. They must be allowed to exist.

For Mexican contemporary artist Bosco Sodi, beauty has never been about absolute control: earth, pigment, texture, fire, time. In Oaxaca, that philosophy helped inspire Fundación Casa Wabi, founded in 2014, where architecture by Tadao Ando creates a dialogue between art, nature and human experience.

The Kanai appointment

Mandarin Oriental has appointed Bosco Sodi as Art Curator and design consultant for Mandarin Oriental Kanai, Riviera Maya. The project envisions commissioned works by more than 20 artists, in conversation with Mayan cultural influences, Asian design philosophy and the surrounding landscape.

  • 680 acres in the Kanai master plan.
  • Approximately 9% allocated to buildings and roads.
  • 49 private branded residences in the current residential offering.

Why it matters

Luxury is evolving from decoration to cultural identity, from possession to experience. An artist's involvement does not guarantee financial appreciation, but authorship, design, hospitality and context are meaningful considerations.

A property can be expensive without being extraordinary. The distinction is worth understanding.
