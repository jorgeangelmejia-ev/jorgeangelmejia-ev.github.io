Distressed or Impaired? Where the Real Opportunity Is

By Jorge Ángel Mejía · 2026-11-14

A discount is not the same thing as an opportunity. Sometimes it is simply a cheaper way to acquire a permanent problem.

The distinction becomes critical when a real-estate market enters a period of selective credit and uneven liquidity.

Curable problems

  • A seller may need cash.
  • A bridge loan may be approaching maturity.
  • A developer may need equity to complete construction.

Problems capital does not cure

Unclear title, unresolved fiduciary claims, defective permits, weak structural demand or impaired exit liquidity are different. Capital does not automatically cure them.

Six filters

  • Legal quality.
  • Capital structure.
  • Completion risk.
  • Micromarket demand.
  • Price versus replacement cost.
  • Exit liquidity.
The opportunity is not simply to buy Tulum at a lower price. It is to identify temporary dislocation before the market distinguishes it from structural risk.
