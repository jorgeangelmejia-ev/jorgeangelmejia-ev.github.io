Tulum Q3 2026: What the Numbers Actually Say

By Jorge Ángel Mejía · 2026-10-02

Tulum's third-quarter numbers call for clear-eyed decisions. Hotel occupancy fell to 15% in August, international arrivals were down 34%, and active short-term rental listings declined 25.7% year over year.

The key figures

  • Building costs run USD 1,000 to 1,222 per m², while asking prices for built space sit at USD 2,330 to 3,055 per m².
  • Average annual short-term rental revenue per listing: USD 17,536, down 13.9% year over year.
  • Active short-term rental listings: 4,024, down 25.7%.

Where the opportunity sits

Lower asking prices and the spread between purchase price and replacement cost are creating unusual negotiating conditions. The opportunity depends on discipline: verify permits and title, underwrite using current rental performance rather than launch projections, and be prepared to hold through a softer season.

A softer market rewards preparation, not bravado.

Frequently asked

Is Tulum real estate cheaper in 2026?
Asking prices have softened in several micromarkets and negotiating conditions have improved, but the spread varies by zone, product and legal quality. Comparable sales, not headlines, should set the price.
How much does it cost to build in Tulum?
Our Q3 2026 data places construction costs at roughly USD 1,000 to 1,222 per m², against asking prices of USD 2,330 to 3,055 per m² for finished space.
