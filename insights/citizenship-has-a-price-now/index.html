Citizenship Has a Price Now. Where Your Family Lives Does Not.

By Jorge Ángel Mejía · 2026-10-04

Argentina just put a price on citizenship: USD 350,000 to the Treasury, or USD 800,000 in a bond. Not one dollar has to go into property.

That is the real story. After Spain closed its property golden visa and the EU's top court struck down Malta's scheme, governments are separating passports from houses.

The new sequence

For mobile families, the order of decisions is now: jurisdiction, then tax structure, then lifestyle, then property. Real estate sits downstream, and that makes the property decision more strategic, not less.

In the Riviera Maya, a home can also support a Mexican residency application. Different tool, different goal. Many families will need both.

I have stopped asking clients "what would you like to buy?" I ask: where does your family need to be in ten years, and where should real estate sit in that plan?
