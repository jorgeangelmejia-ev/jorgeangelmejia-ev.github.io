The Branded Residences Paradox

By Jorge Ángel Mejía · 2026-10-31

What if the greatest risk to your luxury residence in Tulum is not a market correction? What if it is the possibility that what makes your property exceptional today becomes ordinary tomorrow?

A rooftop pool can be replicated. A concierge service can be introduced. A newer development can offer more advanced architecture and technology.

But some combinations of location, privacy, legal certainty and physical characteristics are far more difficult to reproduce.

The question that matters

The question is no longer simply how much a property might appreciate. It is why someone will still choose that property when better-looking alternatives enter the market.

Would you rather own the most impressive residence available today, or one whose advantages are hardest to reproduce tomorrow?
