Wellness Real Estate

Luxury Used to Sell Access. The Next Pitch Is Longevity.

By Jorge Ángel Mejía · 2026-11-08

Sam Nazarian's latest move with The Estate by sbe is a useful signal for real estate: wellness is shifting from an amenity list toward an operating model.

The platform, associated with Nazarian, Tony Robbins, Marc Anthony and Richard Attias, announced branded residences in Coral Gables and Tampa and a resort-residential proposal at Playa Secreta in El Salvador. These are announced plans, with key details still undisclosed for the Florida projects.

How the structure works

  • Hospitality and brand-building from the operator.
  • Visibility, networks and cultural reach from the celebrity partners.
  • Project execution from local development partners.
  • The service layer from longevity and wellness providers.
  • Buyers asked to value the home and the ongoing experience together.

That last point changes the underwriting. When wellness is central to the proposition, buyers need to understand who will operate it, what services are guaranteed, what they cost, and how the brand relationship works over time.

The Tulum context

Tulum already has a range of wellness-oriented propositions. The models differ, and public project descriptions should be checked against contracts, delivery status and operator commitments.

The strongest question is not "How many wellness amenities are included?" It is: "Who is accountable for the experience after the property opens?"
