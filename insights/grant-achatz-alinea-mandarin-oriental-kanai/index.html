The Alinea Effect: Grant Achatz and Mandarin Oriental Kanai

By Jorge Ángel Mejía · 2026-10-17

What does a visionary Chicago chef have to do with the future of luxury residences in Mexico?

For two decades, Grant Achatz and The Alinea Group have challenged the boundaries between gastronomy, art, design and human experience. From The French Laundry to Alinea, from three Michelin stars to an anniversary in Tokyo, the story is about transforming extraordinary service into something unforgettable.

The Riviera Maya chapter

Mandarin Oriental Kanai has announced a partnership with Chef Grant Achatz and The Alinea Group. The concept, WONDERS, promises a different approach to destination gastronomy.

The real question

What happens when architecture, art, hospitality and extraordinary culinary experiences are developed as one connected vision? And what should that mean to someone considering ownership in a branded residence?
