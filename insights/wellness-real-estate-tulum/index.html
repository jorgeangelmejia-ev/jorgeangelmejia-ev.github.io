Wellness Real Estate and the Future of Luxury in Tulum

By Jorge Ángel Mejía · 2026-10-21

We are becoming increasingly conscious of what we put on our bodies. But how conscious are we of the environments in which we live?

Jenny McCarthy's evolution from entertainment to Formless Beauty, a brand built on ingredient awareness and transparency, reflects a shift in consumer culture. It is also where beauty, wellness, hospitality and luxury real estate begin to intersect.

According to the Global Wellness Institute's May 2026 research, wellness real estate reached USD 876 billion in 2025, with projections of USD 1.8 trillion by 2030.

What is wellness real estate?

The intentional design, construction and operation of living environments that support physical, mental and social wellbeing.

Imagine a home where natural light supports your daily rhythm. Where air quality, water management, acoustics and thermal comfort are fundamental architectural decisions rather than afterthoughts. Where wellbeing is not something you purchase during a vacation, but something your residence supports every day.

Wellness marketing is not wellness real estate

  • A jungle setting does not automatically make a building sustainable.
  • A spa does not guarantee healthier living.
  • A beautiful residence does not eliminate the need for environmental, legal and financial due diligence.
For Tulum's next generation of luxury homes, credibility will come from measurable building performance, responsible development and lasting quality of life.
