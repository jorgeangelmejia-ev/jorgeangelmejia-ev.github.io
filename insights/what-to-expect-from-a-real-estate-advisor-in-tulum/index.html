Buying Well

A Beautiful Property Is Not a Decision

By Jorge Ángel Mejía · 2026-10-08

A beautiful property captures attention. The quality of the advice determines whether it deserves consideration.

In a market such as Tulum and the Riviera Maya, the true difference between real estate representation and real estate advisory is not the number of listings presented. It is the quality of the decision-making process.

Five standards

  • 01 Relevance: establish the buyer's objectives before presenting inventory.
  • 02 Evidence: separate asking prices from supported valuation conclusions and clearly disclosed assumptions.
  • 03 Transparency: explain acquisition and recurring ownership costs, restrictions and unresolved questions.
  • 04 Coordination: facilitate independent legal, financial and technical review. Never substitute sales advice for specialized counsel.
  • 05 Accountability: put decisions, responsibilities and next steps in writing.
The quality of the buying experience should be measured by how confidently a buyer can evaluate fit, risks and obligations, not only by how a property looks.

Frequently asked

What should a buyer expect from a real estate advisor in Tulum?
Clear objectives before inventory, evidence-based pricing, transparent ownership costs, coordination with independent legal and technical review, and written accountability for next steps.
