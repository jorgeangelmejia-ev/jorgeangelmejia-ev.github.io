Buying in Tulum? How You Move Your Money Matters

By Jorge Ángel Mejía · 2026-10-24

Buying real estate in Tulum? The purchase price is only part of the equation. Between making an offer and becoming an owner, there is a financial decision many international buyers overlook: how you move your money into Mexico.

Three things deserve attention

  • 01 Exchange rates: the difference between the market rate and the rate offered by your financial institution can affect your purchasing power.
  • 02 International transfer costs: bank charges, intermediary fees and delivery timelines should be understood before funds are committed.
  • 03 Transaction coordination: your exchange provider, bank, legal counsel and closing parties should have consistent, verified payment instructions.

For buyers transferring USD or CAD into Mexico, comparing the total amount received, not simply the advertised exchange rate, can reveal meaningful differences.

The property you choose matters. So does the way you acquire it.

Frequently asked

What should I check before transferring funds to buy property in Mexico?
Compare the total amount received after the exchange rate and all fees, confirm delivery timelines, and verify payment instructions directly with your notary and legal counsel before sending funds.
