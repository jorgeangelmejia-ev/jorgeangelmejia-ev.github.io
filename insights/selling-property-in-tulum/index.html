Your Property Is in Tulum. Your Buyer May Be Anywhere.

By Jorge Ángel Mejía · 2026-10-14

Most property owners ask two questions when deciding to sell: how much is my property worth, and where will it be advertised? Both matter. A third deserves more attention: who is most likely to buy it, and what is our strategy to reach that person?

You may own a villa in Aldea Zamá, a contemporary home in Región 15 or a beachfront residence in Tankah Bay. Your next buyer could be in Mexico City, Miami, Toronto or Vancouver, already working with an advisor they trust.

How the systems work

OMNI MLS facilitates collaboration among participating real estate professionals, and eligible exclusive listings can qualify for distribution through international property portals. Engel & Völkers adds an international network of advisors, relationships and cross-border referrals.

What exposure cannot do

  • Global exposure is not a substitute for a credible asking price.
  • More visibility does not automatically create more qualified buyers.
  • More inquiries do not necessarily produce a transaction.
The advantage comes when market intelligence, presentation, distribution, buyer qualification and negotiation work as one strategy, with one accountable point of representation.
