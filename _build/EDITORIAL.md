# Editorial system — jorgeangelmejia-ev.github.io

Owner: Jorge Ángel Mejía, Sales Manager, Engel & Völkers Tulum.
Goal: organically attract qualified buyers for Tulum / Riviera Maya property through SEO (Google), GEO (AI assistants citing the site) and AEO (direct answers).

## Priority audiences (rotate one per article)
1. Minneapolis–St. Paul: winter second home, direct MSP flights, family use + rental offset.
2. Chicago / North Shore: winter escape, ORD direct flights, design-led villas, branded residences.
3. Canadians (Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary, Montreal): snowbird ownership, CAD→MXN transfers, fideicomiso, rental income and tax questions to raise with advisors.
4. Americans and Canadians working in the Cayman Islands: Caribbean-literate professionals, second coast, beachfront value vs Cayman, asset diversification.
5. Global lifestyle buyers (Mykonos, Ibiza, Miami, Tulum crossover): beachfront estates, Hotel Zone, Casa Carolina / Casa Chic.

## Pillars (rotate)
Market intelligence · Buying well (legal, FX, fideicomiso, costs) · Neighbourhood guides (Aldea Zamá, La Veleta, Región 15, Hotel Zone, Tankah, Akumal) · Branded residences · Wellness real estate · For sellers · Property spotlights (only listings in articles.py LISTINGS).

## Rules for every article
- Title answers a real search question a person from the target audience would type (e.g. "Can Canadians own beachfront property in Tulum?").
- First paragraph gives the direct answer in 2–3 sentences (AEO).
- 600–1,100 words, H2 subheadings phrased as questions where natural, one pull quote, 2–4 FAQ entries (FAQPage schema is generated from `faq`).
- Facts verified by web search at writing time; name the source in the text (e.g. "according to SECTUR", "per the Global Wellness Institute"). Never invent figures, quotes, flights or laws. If unsure, leave it out.
- No legal or tax advice: say "confirm with a Mexican notary / your tax advisor".
- Mention one relevant listing from LISTINGS where it fits naturally, never more than one.
- Voice: Jorge's — measured, evidence-led, advisory, no hype, no exclamation marks.
- English. Slugs: lowercase-hyphenated, include the place keyword.
- Do not repeat a topic already in ARTICLES; check titles first.
