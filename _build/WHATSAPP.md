# NEXUS WhatsApp Playbook (Jorge Mejía · E&V Tulum)

THE RULE — every WhatsApp message ends with exactly TWO questions:
- Q1 open (engagement): invites them to talk about themselves — what they picture, what matters, what worries them. Never yes/no.
- Q2 action (CTA): a choice of three next steps — (a) 15-minute video call (Google Meet or WhatsApp video), (b) private viewing in Tulum / in-person meeting, (c) a lighter step (curated shortlist, brochure, market note).
Format: 2–4 short lines, the lead's language, first name, no emojis except at most one, no links except the property page when relevant, sign "— Jorge". Never two messages without a reply in between (the next one waits for the next Moment). Never false urgency or invented scarcity.

- Viewing → Lofty task "Viewing · {name} · {dates}", tag JM-VIEWING, note the shortlist.
- Lighter step → send the shortlist/brochure/market note; tag JM-CTA-<TYPE>.
