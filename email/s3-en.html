The tax side of selling, before you sign

Dear #lead_first_name#,

In Mexico every property sale is formalised before a notary, and the notary calculates, withholds and pays the seller's income tax on the sale. Knowing the rules early avoids surprises at closing.

  • Non-resident sellers: by default 25% of the gross sale price, with no deductions; alternatively, the tax can be calculated on the net gain at the top rate (currently 35%) when the requirements are met, and the notary applies it.
  • Mexican tax residents: tax is on the gain, after deducting the updated purchase cost, documented improvements, notary fees, appraisals and documented commissions.
  • Primary-home exemption: up to 700,000 UDIs, once every three years, for residents who prove the home is their residence. Foreign owners qualify only as Mexican tax residents, with a fiscal address, RFC or CURP and proof of tax residence.
