Dear #lead_first_name#,

Well-prepared sales close faster and with fewer renegotiations. Two things matter: how the home presents, and how quickly you can answer a serious buyer's due diligence.

  • Your deed (escritura) or, if you own through a bank trust, the trust documents.
  • Current property-tax (predial) and water receipts, and recent condominium or HOA statements where applicable.
  • Plans, permits and invoices for improvements, which also matter for your tax calculation.
  • Small repairs, a deep clean and good light before photography; buyers abroad decide from images first.

Your notary will confirm the final list for your property. I can review what you have and tell you what is missing.
