How foreigners own near the coast

Dear #lead_first_name#,

Mexico's Constitution reserves direct ownership of land within 50 km of the coast to Mexicans, and Tulum is inside that zone. Foreign buyers own securely through one of two routes:

  • A bank trust (fideicomiso): the bank holds title on your behalf and you hold the full rights to use, rent, sell and bequeath the home. The initial term is 50 years and is renewable.
  • A Mexican company, used mainly for non-residential or investment purposes.
  • The trust needs a permit from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (SRE), requested by the bank; the 2026 fee is MXN 21,650 and SRE resolves within five business days.
  • The deed is signed before a notary public, who also verifies title and taxes. In Tulum the acquisition tax (ISAI) is 4% of the property value.

Bank trust fees vary by bank and are not published as a fixed tariff, so I ask for current quotes for each purchase.
